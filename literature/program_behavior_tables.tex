& ACL & 2025

ThrowBench~\cite{prenner2025throwbench}
& arXiv & 2025

& arXiv & 2025

& arXiv & 2025

Demystifying~\cite{abdollahi2025demystifying}

& Develops a human-coded taxonomy of errors in explicit execution traces. \\

& ICLR workshop & 2026
& Separates token-level parsing errors from long-horizon state-tracking failures and studies their relation to inference budget. \\

CodeGlance~\cite{wang2026codeglance}
& ICPC & 2026
& Analyzes output-prediction difficulty using multidimensional code features. \\

CoRE~\cite{gao2026core}
& Findings of ACL & 2026
& Tests intermediate-state correctness across equivalent implementations. \\

RepoReasoner~\cite{wang2026reporeasoner}
& FSE & 2026
& Evaluates cross-file output and call-chain prediction under different context regimes. \\

CodeSense~\cite{roy2026codesense}
& ICLR & 2026
& Derives fine-grained semantic prediction tasks from execution traces of real Python, C, and Java projects. \\

CodeCrash~\cite{lam2026codecrash}
& NeurIPS & 2025
& Stress-tests input and output prediction under structural perturbations and misleading natural-language cues. \\

CodeMind~\cite{liu2024codemind}
& TSE & 2026
& Separates execution, specification, and dynamic-semantics reasoning. \\

Robust Semantics~\cite{spiess2026robustly}
& AIware & 2026
& CruxEval robustness across input perturbations, equivalent program transformations, exceptions, execution-path decisions, and prompt changes. \\

DexBench~\cite{hasanov2026path}
& ACL & 2026
& Evaluates statement-coverage prediction and branch-targeted input mutation on paired execution paths. \\
